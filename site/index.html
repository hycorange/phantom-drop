👻 Phantom Drop

Haunted claim links for stealth TARI on Tari Ootle. Seal an amount into a secret link. Whoever holds the link can open it and receive the funds privately, even with no wallet and zero balance. If nobody opens it before midnight (an expiry epoch), the link dies and only the sender can take the money back.

Why it's spooky

AmountHidden. Pedersen commitment plus range proof.
Owner or recipientNone. Locked by a Merkle root, not a public key.
Claim key, refund key, expiryHidden behind the root until a branch is used.
Message or riddleEncrypted memo, readable only with the link.
SenderNot linkable when funded from stealth UTXOs (--fund utxos).
Zero-funds claimThe claim pays its fee out of the envelope itself (5 TARI in, 4.95 out).
Riddle locksClaim key derived from Argon2id(answer), so a link can be posted publicly without being front-run.
